# 04 — Music / SFX & Assembly

## Music bed (one track under the whole video)
- **Mood:** emotional, cinematic, building — starts mysterious, swells to
  triumphant by scene 7, softens for scene 8.
- **Where to get it (royalty-free, safe for YouTube monetization):**
  - YouTube Audio Library (free) — search "cinematic inspiring," "epic emotional."
  - Pixabay Music (free) — "cinematic," "motivational," "epic."
  - Paid, higher quality: Epidemic Sound, Artlist.
- **Mix level:** keep music **~ -18 to -20 dB under the narrator** so every word
  is clear. Duck the music slightly whenever the narrator speaks (most editors
  have an auto-ducking option).
- **Build:** low/ambient for scenes 1–5, swell at scene 6, peak at scene 7,
  gentle resolve in scene 8.

## SFX stingers (on top of Veo's ambient audio)
Add these 3 accent hits for impact:
1. **Scene 1 (~0:02):** soft magical "whoosh/riser" as the portal opens.
2. **Scene 5 (~0:33):** heavy impact "boom + rubble crash" on the collapse.
3. **Scene 7 (~0:49):** bright "light swell / chime" as the gate opens.
Sources: YouTube Audio Library (SFX), Pixabay SFX, or freesound.org.

---

## Assembly in CapCut (free, easiest) or Premiere

1. **New project, 9:16 vertical, 1080×1920, 30fps.**
2. Import the 8 Veo clips; drop them on the timeline **in order 1→8**. Trim each
   so the full timeline is ~60–64 sec.
3. Add a **cross-dissolve or quick dip-to-black** between scenes (except a hard
   cut into scene 5 for shock). Keep transitions short (~0.3s).
4. **Audio track 2:** place each narrator line on its scene (file 03 timings).
5. **Audio track 3:** music bed across the whole thing; enable ducking.
6. **Audio track 4:** drop the 3 SFX stingers on their frames.
7. **Color (optional polish):** a subtle cinematic LUT; lift scenes 7–8 slightly
   warmer, push scene 5 cooler/darker.
8. **Captions (optional but boosts retention):** auto-caption the narration, big
   bold centered — but do this in the editor, never bake text into Veo clips.

## Export settings
- Resolution **1080×1920**, **30fps** (or 24fps for a filmic look).
- Codec **H.264**, MP4, high bitrate (~15–20 Mbps).
- Audio AAC, 48kHz, stereo.

## Upload as a Short
- Keep it **≤ 60 sec** and 9:16 so YouTube treats it as a Short (trim scene 8 a
  touch if you're over 60s).
- Add **#Shorts** in the title or description.
- Strong first 3 seconds = the portal opening; that's your retention hook.

---

## Quick quality checklist before you post
- [ ] Kai looks like the same person in all 8 clips.
- [ ] No accidental text/subtitles baked into any clip.
- [ ] Every narrator word is clearly audible over the music.
- [ ] The 3 SFX hits land on the right frames.
- [ ] Scene 5 feels like a real low point; scene 7 feels like a real win.
- [ ] Total length ≤ 60s, exported 9:16 1080×1920.
