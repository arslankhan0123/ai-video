# 03 — Narrator Voiceover Script

**Voice:** professional male narrator, deep and warm, cinematic storytelling
tone — think movie-trailer gravitas but intimate. Unhurried; let lines breathe
into the cuts.

**Total runtime target:** ~60–64 sec. Each line sits over its scene. Pauses (…)
are deliberate — don't rush them.

## If you use TTS instead of your own voice

**Recommended:** ElevenLabs (most natural for cinematic narration).
- Voice: a deep male voice such as "Adam," "Antoni," or "Daniel" — pick the one
  that sounds most trailer-like to you.
- Settings: **Stability ~45%**, **Similarity ~80%**, **Style ~25%**, Speaker
  Boost on. Lower stability = more emotion; nudge up if it gets unstable.
- Generate each line separately so you can place it exactly on its scene, and so
  the "…" pauses land right.
- Export as WAV/MP3, 48kHz.

**Free alternatives:** the voice options in CapCut's built-in "Text to speech,"
or Microsoft Edge Read-Aloud voices (Guy / Christopher) captured with Audacity.

## Delivery direction
- Scenes 1–4: measured, curious, building.
- Scene 5: drop lower, slower, heavier — this is the dark moment.
- Scenes 6–7: lift energy and pace, more force.
- Scene 8: warm, resolved; land "Never give up" with quiet conviction, not a
  shout.
