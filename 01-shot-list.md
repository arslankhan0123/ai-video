# 01 — Shot List & Story Arc

**Format:** 9:16 vertical · ~64 sec total · 8 scenes × 8 sec (Veo 3 clip length)
**Style:** realistic cinematic 3D animation, dramatic lighting, detailed
environments, smooth camera moves.
**Logline:** A young man finds a hidden world where every choice reshapes his
future; he's tested, uncovers a buried truth, falls, and rises — learning that
courage and refusal to quit are the real keys.

| # | Time | Beat | What we see |
|---|------|------|-------------|
| 1 | 0:00–0:08 | **Ordinary world** | Kai in a mundane place; a strange glowing doorway appears. Hook. |
| 2 | 0:08–0:16 | **Crossing over** | He steps through into a vast, surreal hidden world — awe. |
| 3 | 0:16–0:24 | **First test** | A yawning chasm / narrow path. Courage over fear. |
| 4 | 0:24–0:32 | **The secret** | Ancient glowing symbols reveal a buried truth. |
| 5 | 0:32–0:40 | **The fall** | A wrong step; the world fractures and collapses around him. Low point. |
| 6 | 0:40–0:48 | **The rise** | Battered, he pushes up and moves forward, jaw set. Determination. |
| 7 | 0:48–0:56 | **Breakthrough** | He overcomes; light floods in, the path completes. Climax. |
| 8 | 0:56–1:04 | **Transformed** | He stands at a summit/overlook, changed, gazing out. Resolve + CTA feel. |

## Pacing notes
- Scenes 1–2 = the **hook** (first 3 sec must grab — portal glow + motion).
- Scene 5 is the **emotional dip** — darkest color, heaviest sound.
- Scenes 6–7 = the **payoff** — brightening palette, rising music.
- Scene 8 lands the lesson; narration delivers the "never give up" button.

## Visual through-line (keep consistent across all clips)
- **Palette:** cool teal-and-slate world, warm amber/gold for the "truth" and
  hope beats (scenes 4, 7, 8).
- **Camera language:** slow push-ins and gentle orbits; one faster move at the
  fall (scene 5) for impact.
- **Keep Kai identical** in every clip — use the CHARACTER BLOCK in file 02.
